SERVICE & MAINTENANCE GALLERY
=============================

HOW TO ADD PHOTOS
-----------------
1. Copy your photos into THIS folder (Asset\service).
   Straight from the phone is fine - they get resized automatically.

2. Go up to the project folder and double-click:  Update Gallery.cmd

3. Say Y when it asks to publish.

That's it. The website updates about a minute later.


HOW TO REMOVE A PHOTO
---------------------
Delete it from this folder, then run Update Gallery.cmd again.


CAPTIONS (optional)
-------------------
Photos work fine with no caption at all. If you want them, make a file
called captions.txt in this folder, one line per photo:

    svc-01.jpg = Full service on an F30 330e | เช็กระยะ F30 330e
    brakes.jpg = Front brake overhaul | เปลี่ยนชุดเบรคหน้า

English goes before the | and Thai after it. You can leave either side
empty. The filenames to use are the ones shown when you run the update -
they get tidied up (lowercase, dashes instead of spaces).


WHAT THE FOLDERS ARE FOR
------------------------
thumbs\      small versions used in the grid - created automatically
_originals\  untouched copies of what you dropped in, kept on your
             computer only. Your safety net - never goes on the website.

Don't edit either by hand.
